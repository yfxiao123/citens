# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be useful and welcoming for everyone — researchers,
students, engineers, and the simply curious. We pledge to keep participation
respectful, constructive, and free of harassment.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps the project:

- citing sources and being precise about what is verified vs. asserted — in
  code, docs, and conversation alike
- asking and answering questions patiently, especially from newcomers
- accepting critique gracefully and critiquing work, not people
- showing your work: reproducible steps, logs, and examples in bug reports

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks
- publishing others' private information without consent
- trolling, insult, or any conduct that would get you escorted out of a
  conference hallway

## Enforcement

Report issues to any project maintainer (see the repository owner's contact
on their GitHub profile). Maintainers will review and respond, and may remove
comments/PRs or ban participants who repeatedly violate these standards.
